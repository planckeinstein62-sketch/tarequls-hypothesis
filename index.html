My Identity

Hi, I’m Tarequl Islam Talha, a physics graduate from National University, Bangladesh.

I created this website as a platform to share my passion for physics, mathematics, and various scientific theories and concepts. Alongside established scientific knowledge, I also explore and present my own theories, ideas, and hypotheses.

My goal is to make fascinating scientific concepts accessible, thought-provoking, and enjoyable for curious minds. I hope you’ll find something here that sparks your curiosity and inspires you to explore the wonders of science.

Welcome, and I hope you enjoy your journey through the world of science!
